TIMeless PHOTO REVIVAL — FREE WEBSITE STARTER

Files
- index.html: a complete, responsive one-page website. It works on phones, tablets, and computers.
- README.txt: these setup and publishing instructions.

FASTEST WAY TO PREVIEW
1. Download and unzip the package.
2. Open index.html in a browser on your computer.
3. Before sharing it with customers, edit index.html in a text editor and replace:
   - "Timeless Photo Revival" with your chosen business name (optional).
   - "YOUR-EMAIL@example.com" in the BUSINESS_EMAIL line with your business/contact email.
4. Replace the decorative gallery placeholders with real before-and-after images only when you have permission to display them.

FREE PUBLISHING OPTION: GITHUB PAGES
1. Create/sign in to a free account at https://github.com/
2. Create a new public repository, for example: timeless-photo-revival
3. Upload index.html to the repository's top level (the repository root).
4. Open the repository's Settings → Pages.
5. Under Build and deployment, select Deploy from a branch, choose the main branch and /(root), then Save.
6. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish and use the website URL shown on that page.
GitHub may take a few minutes to publish. Its free Pages hosting generally requires a public repository for a personal account.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This is a static website; it does not have a server, customer database, payment system, or automatic photo upload.
- The contact form prepares an email in the visitor's email app. It does not silently send or store messages.
- If you later want customers to upload photos, book appointments, or pay online, choose a provider/form service carefully and check its current free-plan limits and privacy terms.
- Avoid promising results before reviewing a photo. Old photos may have missing detail that cannot be recovered.
- Do not upload customers' private family images to a public gallery without their permission.
